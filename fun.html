beetle friend photo booth

Need a hand drawing one? Follow these steps, or skip them and freestyle.

  1. 1. Draw an oval for the body

  2. 2. Draw a curve across the body

  3. 3. Draw a line from the middle of the curve to the bottom of the body

  4. 4. Add some spots

  5. 5. Add some antlers

  6. 6. Use the fill tool to color your beetle friend!
